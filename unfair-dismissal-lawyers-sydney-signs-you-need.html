Make Quote Requests Specific

The inquiry form is described as free to submit with no obligation to proceed, which removes a potential barrier. However, the initial message requires careful consideration. A vague inquiry may result in a vague response, making it challenging to assess the provider's suitability. A focused summary provides the provider a better opportunity to explain their relevance and outline the next steps or required information.

Keep the initial contact factual. State what happened, whether employment has ended, the effective date of dismissal, the relevant state, and the current stage of the matter. If you have supporting documents like a contract, termination letter, or written warnings, mention their availability. Avoid sending extensive private material until a provider requests it and you are comfortable with their process.

Write a short timeline of the workplace issue. Clearly state whether the job ended, is ending, or remains active. List available documents for review. Ask about their process for initial contact, fee discussions, and document review.

Common questions

What are the main types of employment lawyers? The main types are unfair dismissal lawyers (for termination issues), employment contract lawyers (for contract terms and obligations), and workplace investigators (for internal processes and fact-finding).

How should I prepare my initial contact with a lawyer? Prepare a factual summary of what happened, including dates, the state, and current status. Mention any supporting documents you have, such as your contract or termination letter.
